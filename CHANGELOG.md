# 1.0.12

- A tame's damage stays with the player it follows after another player's game takes it over, instead of going to the player it followed before.

# 1.0.11

- The compact meter shows by default: the running fight, then the latest one. A choice made with F10 or in the settings panel still applies. The settings file is written on first launch, with the keys and colours to change.
- A poison's last tick counts towards its damage.
- A fight ends as a kill when its last enemy dies, a retreat or a death stays with its fight, and a blow that Undying prevents counts as healing instead of damage.
- A pet's damage goes to its current handler.
- After rejoining, earlier fights arrive a few at a time instead of in one message that could be too large to arrive at all, and the History tab reads "Fight 1 of 1" instead of "Fight 0 of 1" after Earlier in an empty history.

# 1.0.10

- Dying no longer loses the fight that is running, and a fight that ends in a wipe while you wait to respawn now shows as a wipe.
- Finishing a fight no longer holds a frame while its history is saved, and the meter does less work each frame and each hit.

# 1.0.9

- Fights against minibosses and other renamed bosses show the name their health bar shows instead of the creature they were made from. A long name is shortened with "..." so the fight's status and time stay in sight.

# 1.0.8

- The mod page links the source code and the issue tracker for bug reports.

# 1.0.7

- Fights against Lord Reto and Hildir's minibosses show the creature's name on the meter instead of a raw color tag.

# 1.0.6

- A fight that ends with everyone in it dead now shows as a wipe.

# 1.0.5

- Clearer empty fight histories and empty damage or healing views.
- Combat summaries omit unused tick and overkill fields and label mixed hit and damage-over-time averages accurately.
- Shorter settings labels and help.
